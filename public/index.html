THE EXPERIMENT NOTES

Opinions, with receipts.

One TypeSafe request asks six structured questions: a buy/wait decision and an observation for each available coin. The model sees the past 60 seconds of actual trades, grouped into up to 12 five-second samples, plus the latest price.

“Buy” means a hypothetical entry for the next minute when recent momentum looks sustained. “Wait” means mixed, negative, volatile, or insufficient evidence. Nothing is bought or sold.

Confidence is the model’s reported confidence in its selected label. It is not a calibrated probability of future returns. Reasons are model-selected from a fixed list of observations.

Prices poll every 15 seconds; AI analysis runs about once per minute while visitors are active. With Redis configured, results and the inference cooldown are shared across all server instances. Hidden tabs and paused sessions stop polling. Gaps are not filled with invented prices. Incomplete or stale trade windows are excluded from AI analysis. Failed or stale signals are marked unavailable.

Charts can show 1, 15, or 60 minutes; the AI always uses the most recent 60 seconds of trade samples. The diary lives only in this tab and resets on reload.
